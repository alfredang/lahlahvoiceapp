Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026. This policy covers the NovaVoice apps for iPhone and Mac, provided by Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte Ltd.

The short version

NovaVoice has no accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect, receive or sell your voice, your text or your usage data, and the apps contain no advertising or analytics.

Your voice

NovaVoice uses your microphone to turn speech into text, using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is processed in memory and is not saved. On iPhone, the microphone stays open during a voice session so that the keyboard can begin dictation when you tap Speak. iOS shows this with its microphone indicator, and NovaVoice shows a Live Activity. Audio between dictations is discarded immediately.

Your text

Editing uses Apple Intelligence on your device through Apple's Foundation Models framework. If you keep history turned on, your dictations, notes, dictionary and snippets are stored only on your device. You can delete them in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes them.

The iPhone keyboard and Full Access

The NovaVoice keyboard works for typing without Full Access. Dictation needs Full Access, because that is what lets the keyboard reach the NovaVoice app on your iPhone. The keyboard does not record what you type, and it has no network features.

Accessibility access on Mac

The Mac app uses Accessibility access to detect its hotkeys and paste your text into the app you are using. It checks whether the focused field is a password box so it never pastes there, and when you dictate in a browser it reads the site's address on your Mac to choose a writing style, such as claude.ai. It reads the selected text only when you use a voice edit. This information, and your keystrokes, are not stored or transmitted, except that the site name may appear next to a dictation in your on-device history.

Apple services

Your device may download speech recognition and Apple Intelligence models from Apple. Those downloads are handled by the operating system and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback through WhatsApp or email, we receive what you send and use it only to respond. WhatsApp's own terms apply to messages sent through it.

Children

NovaVoice collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
