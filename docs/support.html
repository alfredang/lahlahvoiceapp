Set up on iPhone

  1. Open LahLah Voice and tap Add the LahLah Voice keyboard.
  2. In Settings, go to General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard and choose LahLah Voice.
  3. Tap LahLah Voice in that list and turn on Allow Full Access. This is only needed for dictation.

Dictate on iPhone

  1. Start a voice session in LahLah Voice, or add its button to Control Center or the Action button, or ask Siri.
  2. In any app, switch to the LahLah Voice keyboard with the globe key.
  3. Tap Speak, talk naturally, and tap again when you're done.

Set up on Mac

  1. Allow the microphone and Accessibility access when asked.
  2. Click into any text box, hold Fn, speak, and let go.
  3. If Fn opens emoji, set “Press 🌐 key to” to “Do Nothing” in System Settings › Keyboard, or choose another key in LahLah Voice Settings.

Why does it say Apple Intelligence is off?

Turn it on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri on a supported device. Without it, LahLah Voice still removes fillers and applies spoken corrections, but does less rewording.
