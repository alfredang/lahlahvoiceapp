Say what you mean, in any app.

NovaVoice turns natural speech into clean, finished text. It removes the ums and false starts, follows your corrections and writes what you intended. Recognition and editing run on your own device with Apple Intelligence.

What it does

  • Understands corrections. Say “Tuesday, no wait, Wednesday” and only “Wednesday” is written.
  • Removes fillers. Ums, stutters and false starts are gone before the text appears.
  • Works where you write. WhatsApp, Telegram, ChatGPT, Mail and any other app with a text field.
  • Learns your words. A personal dictionary for names and jargon, and snippets for text you use often.
  • Keeps your words private. No account, no servers, no tracking.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later, or a Mac with macOS 26 or later. Understanding what you meant uses Apple Intelligence on supported devices. On other devices NovaVoice still removes fillers and applies spoken corrections using built-in rules.
